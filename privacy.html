Privacy policy

Last updated 23 November 2026. This policy covers the Deckhand app for Android (package uk.jj.deckhand) and this website. Deckhand is made by Jonathan Jackson, an individual developer in the United Kingdom, who can be reached by opening an issue on the project's GitHub page.

The short version

  • Your games, stats and settings are kept on your phone. They are never uploaded to the developer or to anyone else.
  • Deckhand never contacts the internet.
  • There are no accounts, no adverts, no analytics and no tracking.
  • When phones are linked, they send each other only what the game needs.

What stays on your phone

Deckhand keeps these in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read:

  • Your games: the deal and the moves, so a game can be carried on.
  • Your stats for each game.
  • Your settings, and the name you choose for playing with friends.

Android's cloud backup and device transfer are switched off for Deckhand, so none of this is copied to a Google account.

Playing with friends

When you host or join a table, the phones send each other only what the game needs: the name each player typed in, the game and its options, the moves, and the locked cards and keys that let each player read their own cards. This goes straight from one phone to another over your own Wi-Fi, a hotspot, a direct Wi-Fi link or Bluetooth. It does not pass through the developer or any server.

Sending the app

Send Deckhand to a friend hands over the app file itself, by your phone's share list or from your phone to theirs over local Wi-Fi. The links to this website in Settings open in your browser, which is covered by the browser's own policy.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays until you delete it. You can remove a game you are part way through, clear your stats in Stats, and uninstalling Deckhand deletes everything the app has kept.

Your rights

Because the developer holds no data about you, there is nothing to request, correct or erase on a server. Everything is already in your hands on your phone.

This website

This site is hosted by GitHub Pages. It uses no cookies, no analytics and no scripts beyond the page itself. GitHub may keep standard web server logs, such as IP addresses, under its own privacy statement.

Children

Deckhand collects nothing from anyone. The poker, brag, pontoon and blackjack games use pretend chips only: there is no money, no betting with anything real and nothing to win or buy. Younger players should ask a parent before linking phones with people they don't know.
