Deckhand 1.0.2

- **Better hints.** In Solitaire the hint now shows the move you would look for first, such as a move that turns a face-down card over or a card from the pile, as long as the game can still be won after it. "Turn over the next card" comes up only when no move on the table would still win.
- TriPeaks hints likewise prefer playing a card from the peaks to turning one over.

**To install:** download `Deckhand.apk` and open it. It installs over the earlier version and keeps your games, stats and settings. Android 8 or later.

Website: https://customgpter.github.io/deckhand/
