What Deckhand does not do

Deckhand has no online play over the internet and no matching with strangers: linked phones need to be on the same Wi-Fi or close together. It has no leaderboards, events or rewards.

Where the others do more

Solitaire by MobilityWare has daily challenges, events and online leaderboards. Microsoft Solitaire Collection has more patience games and links to a Microsoft account. Both update themselves from Google Play.
